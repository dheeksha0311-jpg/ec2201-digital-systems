#### Scene 2: Boolean Variable Design & Digital Formulas (0:45 – 1:30)
- **Visual:** Switch to 'Logic Model' tab.
- **Narrator:**
  "Our system abstracts applicant information into 5 Boolean variables:
   A is income at least ₹40,000; B is credit score at least 700; C is age between 21 and 58; D indicates an active unpaid default; and E represents pledged collateral.
   Our outputs are APPROVE, REJECT, and REVIEW.
   APPROVE is A AND B AND C AND NOT D.
   REJECT is D OR (NOT A AND NOT B) — meaning a default immediately overrides everything, or failing both income and credit leads to auto-rejection.
   REVIEW is the NOR combination of both.
   Notice our algebraic proof: APPROVE multiplied by REJECT is identically zero, guaranteeing mutual exclusivity."

#### Scene 3: Truth Table & SymPy / K-Map Minimization (1:30 – 2:30)
- **Visual:** Switch to 'Truth Table' tab, then to 'K-Map & Proof' tab.
- **Narrator:**
  "Here is our 32-state truth table generated programmatically with 2^5 combinations.
   We used SymPy's Quine-McCluskey SOPform algorithm to compute the minimal Sum-of-Products.
   Here is our formal verification banner: all 32 truth table combinations were programmatically checked against the minimal SOP with zero discrepancies!
   Here is our 4-variable Karnaugh Map showing how minterms group for APPROVE and REJECT."
